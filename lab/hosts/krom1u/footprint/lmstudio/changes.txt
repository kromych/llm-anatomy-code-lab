Footprint of LM Studio on krom1u (macOS 27.2, Apple M1 Ultra), 2026-11-14.
Differences between snapshots taken by code/appB/snapshot.sh: before the
install, after the Homebrew cask, after the headless installer (llmster),
while the daemon, the server and one model ran, after they stopped, after
the cask was removed, and after the remaining files were deleted by hand.
Only the differences are kept; Spotlight workers, apps opened by hand and
the servers of other chapters are left out. Sizes in MB unless stated.

== after removal
  `brew uninstall --zap --cask lm-studio` failed ("Unable to remove some files.
  Please enable Full Disk Access for your terminal"), leaving the app as a
  backup in /opt/homebrew/Caskroom/lm-studio (1.6 GB) and the cask listed;
  `brew uninstall --cask --force lm-studio` completed. ~/.lmstudio,
  ~/.lmstudio-home-pointer and the PATH lines were then removed by hand; the
  final snapshot equals the first.
