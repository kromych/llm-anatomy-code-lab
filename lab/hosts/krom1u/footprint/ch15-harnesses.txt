Chapter 15 harnesses on krom1u (macOS 27, Apple M1 Ultra), 2026-11-14.
Each <harness>/changes.txt lists what changed between three snapshots taken
by code/ch15/footprint.sh: before the install, right after it, and after the
measurement runs. It covers Homebrew packages, top-level entries of ~,
~/.config, ~/.local/*, ~/.cache and ~/Library/..., launchd jobs, listening
sockets, and the files in the harness's own data directories (names and
sizes only, never contents). The full snapshots were discarded; only
the differences are kept, and Spotlight workers and apps opened by hand are
left out of the launchd differences. The harnesses were installed one after
another, so a harness's "added by the runs" also shows the later installs.
