The token: 64 hexadecimal digits, made at each start (the three starts had
three different ones), printed twice in the banner and embedded in the page
that GET / returns. Without it /api/* answers 401; /healthz answers without it.
MCP_INSPECTOR_API_TOKEN sets a token of one's own; DANGEROUSLY_OMIT_AUTH
turns the check off (documentation, not tried). The token is not recorded
here.
